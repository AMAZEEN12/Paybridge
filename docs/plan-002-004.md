## 1. Approach

1. **Migration first, with no new behaviour.** A1 changes `transfers` to `_minor` and moves the mapping with it. The
   existing suite, run with `PAYBRIDGE_FREEZE_TESTS=1`, is the proof nothing broke.
2. **Fake before real.** Payouts and statements are built and proven against fakes. The Flutterwave client (B4) is the
   last payout step because it is the only one that depends on unanswered questions (O1a, O3a, O8, O20, O21, O22).
3. **Small steps, each ending green and committed.** Every step ends with `mvn test` passing (with `paybridge_test`
   present), no edits to an applied migration, and this file updated.
4. **A switch hides unfinished work.** `paybridge.payouts.enabled` and `paybridge.settlement.enabled` default to `false`
   until release, as `paybridge.conversion.enabled` did.
5. **The kobo methods are retired last** (S11), after Paystack payouts no longer depend on anything else changing.

```
Step 0
  |
  A1 --> A2 --> A3 --> A4 --> A5 -----------------------------+
                         |                                    |
                         +--> B1 --> B2 --> B3 --> B4 --> B5 -+--> Z (retire kobo, release)
                         |           |
                         +--> C1 --> C2 --> C3 --> C4 (C2 needs B3's payout references)
```

Track B needs A4 (limits) and A5's quote destination. Track C's positions (C1) need only B1's accounts.

## 4. Track A: Transfers (Intent 002)

### Step A1: `transfers` to `_minor` (the risky step)

**Goal:** same behaviour, new column shape.

- **Migration:** rename `amount_kobo`, `fee_kobo`, `vat_kobo`, `stamp_duty_kobo`, `total_debit_kobo` to `_minor`; add
  `currency` (not null, default `NGN` for existing rows), `target_currency`, `target_amount_minor`, `quote_id`. The
  `type` check stays. Safe on an empty database and on one that holds data.
- **Code:** the `Transfer` mapping, `TransferRepository` queries and every reader move in the **same commit** (the
  reason commit 1 of Plan 001 carried the ledger mapping). `ChargeCalculator` still works in kobo for NGN (B8, R-DAT-3).
- **Tests:** existing suite passes with no change to assertions; new migration test on empty and populated databases;
  existing rows read back with `currency = NGN`.

**Done when:** all pass and the web page still sends a transfer and a Paystack payout by hand.

### Step A2: Recipient lookup (R-LKP)

- `GET /api/v1/recipients/{accountNumber}`; `account` exposes receivable currencies through `AccountApi`.
- Rate limit per customer using `paybridge.resolve-limit-per-minute`; no balances, status or owner id; 404 if unknown;
  a customer can look up their own number.
- **Tests:** acceptance 4; a parked wallet is listed; an excluded currency is not.

### Step A3: Same-currency transfers and notifications (R-XFR-1 to 4, R-NTF-1)

- `POST /api/v1/transfers/internal` takes optional `currency` (default `NGN`); debit and credit the wallets in that
  currency by the same amount through `recordJournal`; stamp duty only for NGN of N10,000 and above; no fee.
- `RECIPIENT_CANNOT_RECEIVE` with the accepted currencies. `TransferEvent` gains `currency` and `amountMinor`; both
  customers are notified.
- **Tests:** acceptance 1, 3, 5 (same-currency part), 6; the existing NGN tests unchanged.

### Step A4: Limits (R-LIM2)

- New method in `compliance/api` for USD-equivalent per-transaction and daily checks; the daily total is the sum of
  `COMPLETED` and `EXECUTING` non-NGN transfers and payouts for the Lagos day; conversions keep their own total.
- Stale rates refuse non-NGN checks only. NGN-to-NGN keeps today's kobo limits and fraud rules.
- **Tests:** acceptance 7 and 21d; the Lagos-day boundary.

- **Departure:** migration `V15` adds `transfers.usd_equivalent_minor` (default 0) so the daily total can be summed
  from the transfer row. A5 copies the quote's USD figure into it. B3 adds payouts to the same total
  (`TransferLimitContext.Kind.PAYOUT`, `payout-max-usd`).

### Step A5: Cross-currency quote, confirm, failure (R-XFR-5 to 10, R-NTF-2 to 5)

- Migration for `quote_destinations` (P2). `TransferQuoteService` beside `QuoteService`; `QuoteMath` reused; the rate is
  read with `RateApi.rateWithUsd()` before any transaction opens.
- Confirm in one transaction: lock the two accounts and wallets in the agreed order, re-check, write the journal of
  spec 7.2, complete the quote. Failure rolls back, marks the quote `FAILED`, sends `NOT_SENT`.
- **Tests:** acceptance 2, 5, 8, 9, 10; two simultaneous confirms; opposite-direction transfers (no deadlock).

**Deploy after A5.**

- **Departures:** migration `V16` creates `quote_destinations` (all spec columns; A5 uses the five it needs).
  The quote lives in `fx` (`TransferQuoteApi`), the confirm in `transfer` (`CrossCurrencyTransferService`).
  `QuoteRepository.moveToExecuting` is now conversion-only, `moveTransferToExecuting` is new, and the conversion
  daily total counts conversions only. Wallet rows are locked by account number, then currency.

## 5. Track B: Payouts (Intent 004)

### Step B1: Ledger accounts, payout journals, corridor table

- Add `EXT:PAYOUT_FEE:<CCY>`, `EXT:RATE_DIFF:<CCY>`, `EXT:REVENUE:<CCY>`, `EXT:TAX_PAYABLE:NGN`, `EXT:IN_FLIGHT:<CCY>`,
  `EXT:PAID_OUT:<CCY>` (non-NGN); reconciliation knows them.
- Journal builders for J1, J3a, J3b, J4, J6 (and J2, J3c, J5 behind the retry switch), each tested to balance in every
  currency on its own.
- The corridor rule table (R-COR-1 to 4): first match wins, no rule means refused, Nigeria-outbound switch.
- **Tests:** the phase balance tests; corridor matching (first match, no match, switch off).

- **Decisions:** same-country pairs (for example NG to NG) are not corridors: the table is not consulted and they are
  allowed, so the Nigeria-outbound switch refuses only NG to elsewhere (R-COR-3). Reconciliation now reports an
  unknown `EXT:` name (R-LED2-4) and counts the per-currency in-flight and paid-out accounts and the payout-fee,
  rate-difference, revenue and tax-payable accounts in "money adds up". Builders live in `ledger.api.PayoutJournals`
  and return a `JournalDraft`; B3 posts one with `ledger.recordJournal(d.key(), d.reference(), d.entryType(), d.lines())`.

### Step B2: Remittance tables, fake gateway, verification

- Migrations for `remittances` and `remittance_attempts`. `PayoutGateway` interface in `remittance/client` and a fake
  that behaves by pattern (success, rejection, timeout; conversion-then-failure only if retries are on).
- Verification (R-VER): `live`, `auto`, `simulated`; deterministic simulated answers (`00` prefix is "not found");
  source stored; cache for 24 hours; refuses to start with simulation and a live key; `GET /payouts/destinations`.
- **Tests:** each `verify-mode`; fallback only on "cannot verify", never on "not found"; acceptance 21b and 21c (with
  the fake standing in for Flutterwave).

- **Departures:** migration `V17` (V16 is `quote_destinations`). `transfer.api.LookupLimitApi`, implemented by
  `ResolveRateLimiter`, lets `remittance` share the lookup limiter (R-VER-7) without importing `transfer` internals.
  `PayoutGateway` already has `getRate`, `initiatePayout` and `verifyPayout` for B3. The fake reports
  converted-but-unpaid only when retries are on. Simulated verification always returns `TEST ACCOUNT <last 4>`
  (acceptance 21c); a typed recipient name, if wanted (R-PAY-2), is a B3 decision.

### Step B3: Payout quote, confirm, settle, webhook (R-PAY, with the fake)

- `POST /payouts/quotes` (fee `max(ceil(1%), $1 equivalent)`, purpose required, destination and verified name locked
  in `quote_destinations`, `OWN_LOCK` rate from the fake); `POST /payouts` reserves (J1), calls the gateway with no
  transaction open, then settles on webhook, verify or job, only on `PENDING`; a timeout stays `PENDING`.
- `POST /api/v1/webhooks/flutterwave` checks the sender first; the scheme is a placeholder behind an interface until O21
  is answered. Failure refunds at once (J4). Read and list endpoints.
- **Tests:** acceptance 12 to 16, 18 to 21; a webhook, a verify and the job racing on one payout (one outcome).

**Deploy after B3 and C4.**

### Step B4: Flutterwave client (blocked until the sandbox is checked)

Before writing it, answer from a test account and the API reference: O1a (does the rate id lock), O1b (extra fee),
O3a (verifiable countries), O8 (sandbox behaviour), O21 (webhook check and base URL), O22 (exact destination amount).

- Implement the client behind `PayoutGateway`; set `verifiable-countries`; choose `rate-mode`; add J6 posting for
  `OWN_LOCK`; implement the real webhook check and the verify-before-settle rule (R-PAY-12a).
- **Tests:** contract tests against recorded samples; acceptance 21a (both modes); the app refuses a live key with a
  simulating mode.

### Step B5: Retries (only if O20 says the sandbox shows converted-but-unpaid)

- Enable `retry.enabled`: J2, retries as new same-currency transfers with a new `reference`, at most 3, then J5.
- **Tests:** acceptance 17. If O20 is no, this step is dropped and B15 stands as written.

## 6. Track C: Settlement (Intent 003)

### Step C1: Positions (R-SET-1, 2, 12)

- `GET /api/v1/admin/settlement/positions`: per currency, credits minus debits from `ledger_entries`, never stored.
  Admin key required; 404 if no key is set. Thresholds read from config (empty by default).
- **Tests:** acceptance 22 and 28 (positions part); sign conventions.

### Step C2: Batches and the fake statement (R-SET-3 to 7)

- Migrations for `settlement_batches`, lines and `partner_statement_lines`. `StatementProvider` port with a fake.
  Scheduled run at `00:30` UTC for the previous UTC day; unique per partner, currency and date; single-flight; catches
  up missed days; immutable once `CLOSED`; no transaction open while a statement is fetched.
- **Tests:** acceptance 23; the UTC midnight edge (00:30 Lagos falls in the previous batch).

### Step C3: Matching and exceptions (R-SET-8 to 10)

- Match by our reference and amount; the five exception types; `PENDING` is `STILL_PENDING` and never refunded; resolve
  with a required note, written to `audit_log`.
- **Tests:** acceptance 24 to 26.

### Step C4: Sweeps (R-SET-11, 13, 14)

- `POST /api/v1/admin/settlement/sweeps`; one balanced journal per currency; system-account sweeps debit the wallet
  through `AccountApi` in the same transaction. Reconciliation stays clean.
- **Tests:** acceptance 27; reconciliation after every sweep and every run.

After B4, add the Flutterwave `StatementProvider` once its list and verify shapes are known (O8); the fake stays for
tests.

## 8. Risks and how the plan answers them

| Risk (spec concern) | Answer |
|---------------------|--------|
| A. Two payments, not one | Phase journals and the timeout rule are proven in B1 and B3 before any real client exists. |
| B. No known rate lock | `OWN_LOCK` default; the difference is isolated in J6 and `EXT:RATE_DIFF`; B4 decides the mode. |
| D. Sandbox not confirmed | Everything is built against the fake first; B4 is blocked until it is checked. |
| G. UTC against Lagos | Limits use the Lagos day, batches the UTC day; the boundary has its own test (A4, C2). |
| H. Sweeping real wallets | C4 posts the journal and the wallet debit in one transaction. |
| I. Retiring kobo methods | Done last, with unchanged assertions as the proof (Z). |
| N. Idempotency across two systems | The transfer `reference` comes from the `Idempotency-Key` on every attempt (B3). |

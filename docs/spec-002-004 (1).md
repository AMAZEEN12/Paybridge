# Spec 002-004: Transfers, settlement, and remittance and payouts abroad

Status: draft      Date: 2026-10-15      Author: Nwafor Blessing
Revision: the payout partner changed from Fincra to Flutterwave (B19), because the Fincra sandbox URL did not respond.
Implements: Intent 002 (`002-cross-border-transfers.md`), Intent 003 (`003-settlement.md`),
Intent 004 (`004-remittance-and-payouts-abroad.md`)
Builds on: Spec 001 (`spec.md`) and Plan 001 (`plan.md`). Spec 001 is finished work; its requirement IDs (R-QUO,
R-CONV, R-FEE, R-LIM, R-LED and so on) are referred to here and are not repeated.

This one spec covers the three intents of the phase because they share one quote, one ledger design and one partner
(Flutterwave). The three parts are written so that a plan can build them as separate tracks. Section 2 lists what this spec
decides on its own (not yet confirmed by you). Section 12 lists what is still open. **Anything marked OPEN must be
answered before the plan is written.**

---

## 1. Summary

After this work:

- **Transfers (Intent 002).** A customer can send money from any active wallet to another PayBridge customer. The sender
  looks up the recipient and sees the currencies the recipient can receive, then picks one. If it is the sender's own
  wallet currency the transfer is **free** and immediate. If it differs, the sender gets a 30-minute **transfer quote**
  (free for the sender's first 30 days, then 0.5% on the FX step) and confirms it. The transfers table becomes `_minor`
  with a currency. Both customers are notified on success; the sender is notified if a confirmed transfer fails.
- **Settlement (Intent 003).** An operator can see a **position report**, a **daily settlement batch** per partner and
  currency (UTC day), matched against partner statements with an **exceptions list**, and can **sweep** accumulated
  fees and taxes to revenue and tax-payable accounts.
- **Remittance and payouts abroad (Intent 004).** A customer can pay a **bank account** through **Flutterwave**: a foreign
  wallet to a Nigerian bank in naira (remittance), a foreign wallet to a bank abroad, or an NGN wallet to a bank abroad.
  The recipient's account is verified through Flutterwave (with a labelled simulated fallback in test mode). The sender gets
  a 30-minute quote (the rate is locked by Flutterwave's rate id if it turns out to lock, otherwise PayBridge locks it
  itself, B16; 1% fee, $1 minimum), gives a purpose, and follows the status. A payout that fails is refunded in full
  in the sender's currency; retries exist only if the sandbox shows a state that needs them (B15, S16). A corridor rule table decides which country pairs are allowed; Nigeria-outbound is on in test mode with a
  switch to turn it off.
- Local NGN-to-NGN payouts through **Paystack** are unchanged.
- The temporary Naira-only code from Intent 001 goes away: the five `LedgerApi` kobo methods and the `Money` helpers
  that nothing needs any more.

A non-programmer can verify the phase end to end: register a UK customer and a Nigerian customer, send 100 USD between
them in the same currency (free), then in different currencies (quote, rate, naira received), pay a Nigerian bank
account from the UK customer's GBP wallet (quote, purpose, status), run the settlement for the day, and see a batch
that matches and an exceptions list that holds only the deliberately broken ones.

## 4. Scope

**In scope**
- Currency-aware transfers between PayBridge customers; the account lookup; the transfer quote; notifications; the
  `transfers` migration; retiring the kobo ledger methods.
- Settlement: positions, daily batches, statement matching, exceptions, sweeps, operator endpoints.
- Flutterwave payouts in three flows; the corridor rule table; payout quote, purpose, limits, retry and refund; the Flutterwave
  webhook; the fake Flutterwave gateway.
- Flyway migrations `V14` onward.

**Out of scope**
- Fraud rule changes (B6). An FX margin on transfer quotes. Collecting money from senders (cards, bank, mobile money).
- Guest senders, cash pickup, mobile-money wallets, domiciliary accounts.
- Replacing Paystack for local NGN-to-NGN payouts.
- Admin or customer dashboards, email and SMS, HTTP rate limiting beyond the lookup limiter, monitoring.
- Real money, live Flutterwave credentials, KYB, licensing, and tax filing.

## 5. Glossary

- **Source currency / receive currency:** the currency of the sender's wallet; the currency the recipient gets.
- **Cross-currency transfer:** a transfer where the two differ. It needs a quote.
- **Payout:** a payment to a bank account through Flutterwave. A **remittance** is a payout from a foreign wallet to a
  Nigerian bank in naira.
- **Corridor:** a sender country to destination country pair. The **corridor rule table** says which are allowed.
- **Partner:** an outside party PayBridge settles with: `PAYSTACK`, `FLUTTERWAVE`, or the internal `FX_POOL`.
- **Position:** what an account holds, worked out from the ledger as credits minus debits.
- **Batch:** one partner and one currency for one UTC day.
- **Statement line:** one payout as the partner reports it. **Exception:** a batch line that does not match.
- **Sweep:** a journal that moves accumulated fees or taxes to revenue or tax-payable.
- **Rate lock:** the rate and fee of a quote do not change from confirm to completion. `PROVIDER_LOCK` means Flutterwave's rate id holds the rate; `OWN_LOCK` means PayBridge holds it and carries any difference.

## 6. Requirements

### 6.1 Account lookup (R-LKP)

- **R-LKP-1 (MUST)** `GET /api/v1/recipients/{accountNumber}` returns the recipient's display name and the list of
  currencies they can receive (every wallet, active or parked, whose currency is not excluded). It returns 404 for an
  unknown account. A frozen account can still receive money today (only debits are refused), so its wallets are listed.
- **R-LKP-2 (MUST)** The lookup is limited per customer per minute (S4, reuse the setting
  `paybridge.resolve-limit-per-minute`). It never returns balances, wallet status or the account number's owner id.
- **R-LKP-3 (MUST)** A customer can look up their own account number; the result lists their own wallets.

### 6.2 Transfers (R-XFR)

- **R-XFR-1 (MUST)** **Same currency.** `POST /api/v1/transfers/internal` accepts an optional `currency` (default
  `NGN`). The sender's wallet in that currency is debited and the recipient's wallet in that currency is credited by
  the same amount. No PayBridge fee. The `Idempotency-Key` rules are unchanged.
- **R-XFR-2 (MUST)** The recipient must have a wallet in the currency (active or parked). If not, the transfer is
  refused with `RECIPIENT_CANNOT_RECEIVE` and a message naming the accepted currencies.
- **R-XFR-3 (MUST)** The sender's wallet must be active and the account not frozen; the PIN rules and the fraud rules
  for NGN-to-NGN are unchanged.
- **R-XFR-4 (MUST)** The N50 stamp duty applies to an NGN transfer of N10,000 and above, as today. It is the only
  charge on a same-currency transfer. Other currencies carry no stamp duty.
- **R-XFR-5 (MUST)** **Cross currency.** `POST /api/v1/transfers/cross-currency/quotes` takes the recipient account,
  `sourceCurrency`, `receiveCurrency` and the source amount, and returns a `TRANSFER` quote (type, rate, fee and reason,
  exact receive amount, recipient name, expiry in 30 minutes). `POST /api/v1/transfers/cross-currency` confirms by
  quote id, with the PIN and an `Idempotency-Key`.
- **R-XFR-6 (MUST)** Quote maths follow R-CONV-2: `fee = ceil(source x fee rate)` in the source currency (0 during the
  sender's first 30 days, reason `PROMO`, otherwise `STANDARD`); `net = source - fee`; `receive = floor(net x rate x
  10^(receive decimals - source decimals))`. A receive amount of 0 is rejected.
- **R-XFR-7 (MUST)** A transfer quote is single use and its rate and fee are locked from confirm to completion
  (R-QUO-3 to R-QUO-6). Confirming runs in one database transaction that locks the two accounts and the wallets in the
  one agreed order, re-checks balance, wallet status, recipient wallet, frozen state and limits, writes the journal
  (section 7.2), and completes the quote. Any failure rolls back and marks the quote `FAILED`.
- **R-XFR-8 (MUST)** The rate is read with `RateApi.rateWithUsd()` **before** any transaction is opened.
- **R-XFR-9 (MUST)** Every completed transfer records its quote, journal key, currencies and amounts and is audit
  logged.
- **R-XFR-10 (MUST)** The sender and the recipient can both see the transfer in their history, with the currency.

### 6.3 Limits for transfers and payouts (R-LIM2)

- **R-LIM2-1 (MUST)** NGN-to-NGN transfers keep the existing limits and fraud rules in kobo (B5, B6).
- **R-LIM2-2 (MUST)** Every other transfer and every payout is checked against a per-transaction maximum and a
  per-customer daily maximum in USD equivalent (placeholders $1,000 and $5,000), at quote time and again at confirm
  time. The USD equivalent comes from the same snapshot as the quote and is stored on it.
- **R-LIM2-3 (MUST)** The daily total is the sum of the customer's `COMPLETED` and `EXECUTING` non-NGN transfers and
  payouts for the Lagos day (S10, S12). Conversions keep their own total.
- **R-LIM2-4 (MUST)** The check is a new method on the `compliance` module's `api/` package; the transfer and
  remittance modules call it and never read compliance tables.
- **R-LIM2-5 (MUST)** When rates are stale, these checks refuse with the existing "rates temporarily unavailable"
  message. NGN-to-NGN is not affected.

### 6.4 Notifications (R-NTF)

- **R-NTF-1 (MUST)** A completed same-currency or cross-currency transfer creates a notification for the sender and one
  for the recipient, each showing the currency and the amount that customer sent or received.
- **R-NTF-2 (MUST)** A confirmed cross-currency transfer that fails creates one notification for the sender. It says the
  transfer did not go through and that no money moved. It does not say "refunded".
- **R-NTF-3 (MUST)** A request refused before confirming (a limit, an unsupported currency, a stale rate) creates no
  notification; the API error is the answer.
- **R-NTF-4 (MUST)** Notifications stay after-commit and asynchronous, and read events from the owning module's `api/`
  package. They never call back into `transfer` or `remittance`.
- **R-NTF-5 (MUST)** `TransferEvent` carries `currency`, `amountMinor` and, for a cross-currency transfer, the receive
  currency and amount. A new stage `NOT_SENT` reports a confirmed transfer that failed.
- **R-NTF-6 (SHOULD)** Payout events carry the same notifications as Paystack payouts today (accepted, successful,
  failed and refunded), made currency-aware (section 6.7).

### 6.5 Corridors (R-COR)

- **R-COR-1 (MUST)** A config table `paybridge.corridors` lists rules of the form `from` country, `to` country (or `*`)
  and `enabled`. The first matching rule wins; a corridor with no rule is **refused**.
- **R-COR-2 (MUST)** The sender's country is the stored customer country. The destination country is the bank's
  country for a payout.
- **R-COR-3 (MUST)** The shipped default has Nigeria-outbound **enabled** (B13). Setting that one rule to `false`
  refuses every payout whose sender country is `NG` and whose destination is not `NG`, with `CORRIDOR_NOT_ALLOWED`.
  Remittance to Nigeria from another country is a separate rule and is not affected.
- **R-COR-4 (MUST)** The rule is checked at quote time and again at confirm time, so switching it off stops quotes
  already issued.
- **R-COR-5 (OPEN)** Whether the same table governs PayBridge-to-PayBridge transfers across borders (O7). Until it is
  answered, transfers are not checked against it.

### 6.6 Payouts (R-PAY)

- **R-PAY-1 (MUST)** A customer can pay a bank account in a supported destination country in the destination's
  currency, in the three flows of B12. The recipient needs no PayBridge account.
- **R-PAY-2 (MUST)** The sender supplies the destination country, the bank details that country needs, the recipient
  type (individual or corporate), the amount and the **purpose of transfer** from a config list. All are required. The
  recipient's name comes from verification (R-VER-1); a typed name is used only when verification is simulated. Bank
  detail fields come from a per-country config (O4).
- **R-PAY-3 (MUST)** `POST /api/v1/payouts/quotes` returns a `TRANSFER` quote with the source and payout currencies,
  amounts, rate, fee and reason `STANDARD`, the exact amount the recipient's bank will receive, the destination (masked
  account, verified name and where it came from, R-VER-3), and the expiry. The expiry is **30 minutes, or Flutterwave's own
  rate expiry if the rate is provider-locked and that is shorter** (B16). The destination, verified name and purpose are locked into the quote
  (`quote_destinations`).
- **R-PAY-3a (MUST)** **Rate source (B16).** When the payout needs a conversion, PayBridge asks Flutterwave for a rate
  (`POST /transfers/rates`) at quote time. Two modes, set by `paybridge.payouts.flutterwave.rate-mode`:
  - `PROVIDER_LOCK` (only after the API reference confirms the rate id locks): the quote's rate and recipient amount
    are Flutterwave's, its expiry caps ours, and its rate id is stored (`provider_rate_ref`) and sent with the payout.
  - `OWN_LOCK` (default): the quote's rate is the one Flutterwave returned at quote time, and PayBridge locks it for
    30 minutes. The recipient amount is `floor(net x rate)` in the payout currency's minor units and is what PayBridge
    promises. If Flutterwave's real cost at payout time is worse, PayBridge carries the difference (S15, R-PAY-7a).
  The mid-market snapshot (`RateApi.rateWithUsd`) still supplies the **USD equivalent** for the limits and the $1
  minimum, and is stored on the quote.
- **R-PAY-3b (MUST)** If the Flutterwave rate cannot be obtained (unavailable, corridor not supported), the quote is
  refused with a clear message. No payout quote falls back to the mid-market rate, because the "exact amount received"
  would no longer be true.
- **R-PAY-4 (MUST)** Fee: `max(ceil(source x 1%), ceil($1 equivalent in the source currency))`, rounded up, in the source
  currency. There is no free period (S5). The $1 equivalent uses the quote's USD rate.
- **R-PAY-5 (MUST)** The amount converted is `net = source - fee`. The payout amount is Flutterwave's destination amount
  for `net` in `PROVIDER_LOCK` mode, and `floor(net x rate)` in `OWN_LOCK` mode (R-PAY-3a). A payout amount of 0 is
  rejected. When the source and payout currencies are the same, the rate is 1, no conversion is involved, and the payout
  amount is `net`.
- **R-PAY-6 (MUST)** `POST /api/v1/payouts` (quote id, PIN, `Idempotency-Key`) confirms. It follows the pattern of the
  existing payout: **reserve** (one transaction: lock, re-check, debit, write phase J1, save the payout `PENDING`,
  commit), then call Flutterwave with **no transaction open**, then **settle** in a short transaction.
- **R-PAY-7 (MUST)** The Flutterwave transfer request carries our own `reference`, derived from the `Idempotency-Key`,
  so a retry can never pay twice, and, in `PROVIDER_LOCK` mode, the stored rate id. In `OWN_LOCK` mode it carries the
  exact destination amount (O22).
- **R-PAY-7a (MUST)** In `OWN_LOCK` mode, once the payout succeeds and Flutterwave's real source-currency cost is known,
  any difference from the quoted net is posted by journal J6 and shown in the settlement position. It never changes the
  customer's debit or the amount the recipient gets.
- **R-PAY-8 (MUST)** The outcome comes from the Flutterwave webhook, from the verify-by-reference call, and from a scheduled
  job for payouts still `PENDING`. The settle step locks the payout row and acts only on a payout that is still
  `PENDING` or `RETRYING`, so a webhook, a verify and the job can race and each outcome is applied once.
- **R-PAY-9 (MUST)** A **timeout is not a failure**. The payout stays `PENDING`, nothing is refunded, and the status is
  re-queried. This matches the existing Paystack rule.
- **R-PAY-10 (MUST)** **Failure.** A payout that Flutterwave reports as failed is refunded to the sender in full in the
  source currency, at once, fee included (phase J4, S6). The payout is `FAILED` and the quote `FAILED`.
- **R-PAY-11 (MAY, off by default)** If `paybridge.payouts.retry.enabled` is `true` (S16, O20), a failure after money has
  been converted but not paid keeps the converted money, records the conversion (J2), and retries as a **new**
  same-currency payout with a new `reference`, never replaying the original request, at most **3** times with
  configurable spacing (O5). If the third retry fails, refund the sender in the source currency, full amount including
  the fee (J5), and mark the payout `FAILED`. With retries off none of this runs.
- **R-PAY-12 (MUST)** The Flutterwave webhook endpoint `POST /api/v1/webhooks/flutterwave` checks the sender before doing
  anything. The scheme (a `verif-hash` header compared with a secret hash you choose, or a signature over the payload) is
  confirmed against the current API reference (O21). A wrongly signed or unsigned call is refused, and the comparison is
  constant-time. The secret comes from an environment variable and is never logged.
- **R-PAY-12a (SHOULD)** A webhook is treated as a hint: before settling, the payout is confirmed by the verify-transfer
  call, so a forged or replayed webhook cannot move money.
- **R-PAY-13 (MUST)** Limits (R-LIM2) and the corridor rule (R-COR) are checked at quote time and again at confirm time.
- **R-PAY-14 (MUST)** Each payout is audit logged at reserve, conversion, each retry, success and refund, with the
  account number masked.
- **R-PAY-15 (MUST)** The sender can read a payout by reference and list their payouts. The status shown is `PENDING`,
  `SUCCESSFUL` or `FAILED` (S14).
- **R-PAY-16 (MUST)** `GET /api/v1/payouts/destinations` returns the supported countries, their currencies, the bank
  fields each needs, and the purpose list. The supported currencies at the start come from Flutterwave's documented list
  intersected with the currency table and the exclusion list (O6).
- **R-PAY-17 (MUST)** The recipient's account is verified before a quote is issued (R-VER).

### 6.6a Account verification (R-VER)

- **R-VER-1 (MUST)** `POST /api/v1/payouts/verify-account` (country, bank details, currency) asks Flutterwave to verify the
  account and returns the **account name Flutterwave reports** plus the **verification source**. The quote and the payout use
  that real name, not the name the sender typed. The sender's typed name is kept only if verification is simulated.
- **R-VER-2 (MUST)** Verification has a mode, `paybridge.payouts.flutterwave.verify-mode`, mirroring the Paystack
  `resolve-mode`:
  - `live`: Flutterwave answers; any failure refuses the quote.
  - `auto` (default): Flutterwave answers where it can; if the corridor is not supported, Flutterwave is unavailable, or the
    configured allowance is spent, a **simulated** answer is returned instead.
  - `simulated`: always simulated.
  `auto` and `simulated` are allowed **only with a Flutterwave test key**. The app refuses to start with a live key and a
  simulating mode, as it does for Paystack with a live key.
- **R-VER-3 (MUST)** Every verification result carries a **source**: `FLUTTERWAVE` (real) or `SIMULATED`. The source is stored
  on the quote destination and shown to the sender: a simulated name is labelled "name not verified (test mode)" and
  looks different from a real one. A simulated verification is never recorded as real, in the audit log or in
  settlement.
- **R-VER-4 (MUST)** The simulated answer is deterministic, like the fake Paystack gateway: the account number decides the
  outcome (an account number starting with `00` is "not found"; any other returns `TEST ACCOUNT <last 4 digits>`), so
  tests and demos can reach every path.
- **R-VER-5 (MUST)** Names already verified are remembered in memory for a configured time (default 24 hours), so the
  lookup and the payout do not spend two allowances. A configured daily allowance (if Flutterwave or the sandbox limits
  lookups) stops the app from calling Flutterwave once spent and falls back as `auto` requires. Neither the cache nor the
  allowance logs a full account number.
- **R-VER-6 (MUST)** A real verification that says the account does not exist refuses the quote. It does **not** fall
  back to a simulated name. Only "cannot verify" (unsupported, unavailable, spent) falls back.
- **R-VER-7 (MUST)** Verification calls are rate limited per customer per minute (the lookup limiter of R-LKP-2), because
  they reveal who owns an account.
- **R-VER-8 (SHOULD)** Which corridors Flutterwave can verify is read from configuration, so a corridor that cannot be
  verified goes straight to the fallback without a wasted call (O3a).

### 6.7 Notifications for payouts (R-NTF continued)

- **R-NTF-7 (SHOULD)** Accepted (debited and sent, outcome not known), successful, and failed-with-refund messages, as
  for Paystack payouts today, in the right currency. A payout that is retrying shows only the accepted message until it
  ends. (O9)

### 6.8 Settlement (R-SET)

- **R-SET-1 (MUST)** **Positions.** `GET /api/v1/admin/settlement/positions` returns, per currency, the position of
  `EXT:FX_POOL:<CCY>`, `EXT:FX_FEE:<CCY>`, `EXT:PAYOUT_FEE:<CCY>`, `EXT:RATE_DIFF:<CCY>`, `EXT:IN_FLIGHT:<CCY>`, `EXT:PAID_OUT:<CCY>`
  (and the NGN names), the system accounts (`9000000001`, `9000000002`, `9000000003`), `EXT:REVENUE:<CCY>` and
  `EXT:TAX_PAYABLE:NGN`. A position is credits minus debits from `ledger_entries`; it is never stored or typed in.
- **R-SET-2 (MUST)** Each pool has a warning threshold in config (O11). A position below its threshold is logged as a
  warning and flagged in the report. It never blocks a customer.
- **R-SET-3 (MUST)** **Batches.** A scheduled run builds, for each partner and currency, a batch for the previous UTC
  day: the payouts, transfers and conversions it covers, our total, the partner's total, a count, the UTC start and end,
  and a status (`OPEN`, `CLOSED`, `SETTLED`, `EXCEPTION`).
- **R-SET-4 (MUST)** The run is **idempotent**: a batch is unique per partner, currency and UTC date, so running a day
  twice (including after a restart) never double counts. It is single-flight and catches up days missed while the host
  was asleep.
- **R-SET-5 (MUST)** A batch is **immutable once `CLOSED`**. A correction is an adjustment journal that belongs to the
  next batch.
- **R-SET-6 (MUST)** **Statements.** A `StatementProvider` interface lists a partner's payouts for a UTC date range
  (reference, amount, currency, status) and returns them paged. A fake serves tests and local runs; the Paystack
  (List Transfers by date range, Verify Transfer) and Flutterwave (list transfers and verify transfer by reference; a balance statement if one exists) implementations come when the partner's real shape is confirmed (O8). Nothing in tests or local
  runs calls a real partner.
- **R-SET-7 (MUST)** No database transaction is open while a statement is fetched. Fetch, store the statement lines,
  then match in a short transaction.
- **R-SET-8 (MUST)** **Matching** uses our payout reference and the amount, never names. Each of our payouts becomes
  matched or an exception of one of these types: `MISSING_AT_PARTNER`, `MISSING_ON_OUR_SIDE`, `AMOUNT_DIFFERS`,
  `STATUS_DIFFERS`, `STILL_PENDING`.
- **R-SET-9 (MUST)** A payout still `PENDING` or `RETRYING` is reported as `STILL_PENDING` and is **never refunded**
  because a batch closed. A payout the partner reports later than the batch day is not an exception until the
  configured lag has passed (O10).
- **R-SET-10 (MUST)** **Exceptions.** An operator can list open exceptions and mark one resolved with a required note
  (`POST /api/v1/admin/settlement/exceptions/{id}/resolve`). The resolution is stored on the exception and written to
  `audit_log` with who and why. Nothing is edited or deleted.
- **R-SET-11 (MUST)** **Sweeps.** `POST /api/v1/admin/settlement/sweeps` (an operator action, S13) moves the balance of
  a fee or tax account to its destination in one balanced journal per currency: the NGN fee, VAT and stamp-duty
  accounts to `EXT:REVENUE:NGN` and `EXT:TAX_PAYABLE:NGN`; `EXT:FX_FEE:<CCY>` and `EXT:PAYOUT_FEE:<CCY>` to
  `EXT:REVENUE:<CCY>`. The system accounts hold real NGN wallet balances, so their sweep debits the wallet through
  `AccountApi` in the same transaction as the journal.
- **R-SET-12 (MUST)** All settlement endpoints require the `X-Admin-Key` header and are off (404) if no key is set, like
  the existing reconciliation endpoint. A separate setting holds this key.
- **R-SET-13 (MUST)** Reconciliation (ledger against wallets) and settlement (ledger against partners) are separate
  checks. Both can be run on demand, and reconciliation stays clean after every sweep and every settlement run.
- **R-SET-14 (SHOULD)** A batch shows its UTC start and end, because the Lagos clock is one hour ahead.

### 6.9 Ledger (R-LED2)

- **R-LED2-1 (MUST)** Every journal balances **per currency** (R-LED-3). The per-account-per-side uniqueness
  (`uq_ledger_line`) stays, so a journal never lists the same account twice on one side.
- **R-LED2-2 (MUST)** Transfers, payouts, conversions and sweeps all post through `recordJournal` (or a thin wrapper
  that calls it).
- **R-LED2-3 (MUST)** By the end of the phase the five kobo methods (`recordFunding`, `recordInternal`,
  `recordReserve`, `recordPayoutSuccess`, `recordPayoutRefund`) are re-implemented through `recordJournal` with currency
  `NGN` and **identical journal shapes**, then deleted. The existing reconciliation and Paystack payout tests must pass
  with no change to their assertions (S11).
- **R-LED2-4 (MUST)** Reconciliation includes the new `EXT:` accounts per currency and reports a problem for an unknown
  account, as it does today.

### 6.10 Data and migration (R-DAT)

- **R-DAT-1 (MUST)** `transfers` columns `amount_kobo`, `fee_kobo`, `vat_kobo`, `stamp_duty_kobo`, `total_debit_kobo`
  become `_minor`. New columns: `currency` (not null, default `NGN` for existing rows), `target_currency`,
  `target_amount_minor`, `quote_id`. The `type` check stays (`INTERNAL`, `EXTERNAL`).
- **R-DAT-2 (MUST)** The migration is safe on an empty database and on one that holds data, and the `Transfer` mapping,
  repositories and queries move together with it (the same reason commit 1 of Plan 001 step 1 carried the mapping).
- **R-DAT-3 (MUST)** Charge settings (`paybridge.charges`) and fraud thresholds (`paybridge.fraud`) keep `_kobo` and
  apply to NGN only (B8).
- **R-DAT-4 (MUST)** Flyway owns the schema. Migrations are new files numbered above `V13`, assigned when a branch
  merges (D4 of Plan 001). `ddl-auto` stays `validate`.

## 7. Design

### 7.1 Data model (suggested; the plan can adjust)

**quotes** (exists, V13). No column changes for transfers beyond what a quote already has. A transfer or payout quote
uses `type = TRANSFER`. A same-currency payout has `rate = 1` and still stores a snapshot for the USD equivalent.

**quote_destinations** (new): `quote_id` (PK, FK to `quotes`), `kind` (`PAYBRIDGE_ACCOUNT`, `BANK_ACCOUNT`),
`account_number`, `account_name`, `bank_code`, `bank_name`, `country` (2 letters), `currency`, `bank_fields` (the extra
per-country fields, for example IBAN or sort code, as text), `recipient_type` (`INDIVIDUAL`, `CORPORATE`), `purpose`,
`verification_source` (`FLUTTERWAVE`, `SIMULATED`), `rate_mode` (`PROVIDER_LOCK`, `OWN_LOCK`), `provider_rate_ref`, `provider_rate_expires_at`. The destination,
the verified name and its source are fixed at quote time and cannot change at confirm.

**transfers** (exists): changes in R-DAT-1.

**remittances** (new, module `remittance`): `id`, `reference` (unique), `customer_id`, `quote_id` (unique),
`source_account_number`, `source_currency`, `payout_currency`, `source_amount_minor`, `fee_minor`,
`payout_amount_minor`, `purpose`, `status` (`PENDING`, `RETRYING`, `SUCCESSFUL`, `FAILED`), `conversion_done`
(boolean, used only with retries on), `attempts` (0 to 3, retries only), `next_retry_at`, `rate_difference_minor` (nullable, `OWN_LOCK`), `provider` (`FLUTTERWAVE`), `provider_payout_ref`, `provider_status`,
`failure_reason`, `idempotency_key`, `request_hash`, `version`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, `completed_at`.
`UNIQUE (source_account_number, idempotency_key)`. `balance` and `status` checks as for `transfers`.

**remittance_attempts** (new): `id`, `remittance_id`, `attempt` (0 for the original), `customer_reference` (unique),
`provider_payout_ref`, `result`, `created_at`. One row per request sent to Flutterwave.

**settlement_batches** (new): `id`, `partner`, `currency`, `period_date` (UTC date), `period_start`, `period_end`,
`status`, `item_count`, `our_total_minor`, `partner_total_minor`, `created_at`, `closed_at`, `settled_at`.
`UNIQUE (partner, currency, period_date)`.

**settlement_batch_lines** (new): `batch_id`, `reference`, `kind` (`PAYOUT`, `CONVERSION`, `TRANSFER`), `amount_minor`,
`our_status`, `partner_status`, `match_result`.

**partner_statement_lines** (new, append-only in practice): `id`, `partner`, `currency`, `reference`, `amount_minor`,
`status`, `partner_time`, `fetched_at`, `batch_id`.

**settlement_exceptions** (new): `id`, `batch_id`, `reference`, `type`, `detail`, `status` (`OPEN`, `RESOLVED`),
`resolved_by`, `resolution_note`, `resolved_at`.

**sweeps** (new): `id`, `source_account`, `currency`, `amount_minor`, `destination_account`, `journal_key`,
`created_by`, `created_at`.

All amounts are `BIGINT` minor units; rates are `NUMERIC(24,12)`. Ledger account names stay within the 20-character
column (`EXT:TAX_PAYABLE:NGN` is 19). Journal keys stay within 80.

### 7.2 Journals

Notation: account `A` (sender), `B` (recipient), source currency `S`, other currency `T`, amount `X` debited, fee `F`,
net `N = X - F`, received `Y`. A line is `account, side, currency, amount`.

**Same-currency transfer (`XFER:<reference>`).** `A:S DEBIT X (+ stamp duty)`, `B:S CREDIT X`, and for NGN of N10,000
and above `9000000003:NGN CREDIT stamp duty`. Each currency balances.

**Cross-currency transfer (`XFER:<quote_id>`).**

| Line | Account | Side | Currency | Amount |
|------|---------|------|----------|--------|
| 1 | `A:S` | DEBIT | S | X |
| 2 | `EXT:FX_POOL:S` | CREDIT | S | N |
| 3 | `EXT:FX_FEE:S` | CREDIT | S | F (omit if 0) |
| 4 | `EXT:FX_POOL:T` | DEBIT | T | Y |
| 5 | `B:T` | CREDIT | T | Y |

This is the conversion journal of Spec 001, section 7.2, with the credit going to the recipient's wallet.

**Payout phases (journal keys `PAYOUT:<reference>:J1` to `:J6`).**

| Phase | When | Lines |
|-------|------|-------|
| **J1 reserve** | on confirm | `A:S DEBIT S X`; `EXT:IN_FLIGHT:S CREDIT S X` |
| **J3a success, no conversion** | payout paid, `S` equals `T` | `EXT:IN_FLIGHT:S DEBIT S X`; `EXT:PAID_OUT:S CREDIT S N`; `EXT:PAYOUT_FEE:S CREDIT S F` |
| **J3b success, with conversion** | payout paid, `S` is not `T`, retries off | `EXT:IN_FLIGHT:S DEBIT S X`; `EXT:FX_POOL:S CREDIT S N`; `EXT:PAYOUT_FEE:S CREDIT S F`; `EXT:FX_POOL:T DEBIT T Y`; `EXT:PAID_OUT:T CREDIT T Y` |
| **J4 refund** | payout failed (retries off, or no conversion was done) | `EXT:IN_FLIGHT:S DEBIT S X`; `A:S CREDIT S X` |
| **J6 rate difference** | `OWN_LOCK` only, after success, real cost differs by `D` in `S` | worse than quoted: `EXT:RATE_DIFF:S DEBIT S D`; `EXT:FX_POOL:S CREDIT S D`. Better than quoted: the two sides swap. |
| **J2 conversion done** | retries on only: `S` is not `T`, converted but not paid | `EXT:IN_FLIGHT:S DEBIT S X`; `EXT:FX_POOL:S CREDIT S N`; `EXT:PAYOUT_FEE:S CREDIT S F`; `EXT:FX_POOL:T DEBIT T Y`; `EXT:IN_FLIGHT:T CREDIT T Y` |
| **J3c success after J2** | retries on only | `EXT:IN_FLIGHT:T DEBIT T Y`; `EXT:PAID_OUT:T CREDIT T Y` |
| **J5 refund after J2** | retries on only: third retry failed | `A:S CREDIT S X`; `EXT:FX_POOL:S DEBIT S N`; `EXT:PAYOUT_FEE:S DEBIT S F`; `EXT:IN_FLIGHT:T DEBIT T Y`; `EXT:FX_POOL:T CREDIT T Y` |
Each phase balances in each currency on its own. With retries on, after J5 `EXT:FX_POOL:T` holds `Y` of the destination currency:
this is the leftover position that settlement reports. A retry writes no journal; only its outcome does. For the
NGN names before the kobo methods are retired, `EXT:IN_FLIGHT` and `EXT:PAID_OUT` stand for the NGN versions (S7).

**Sweep (`SWEEP:<id>`).** From `EXT:FX_FEE:S`: `EXT:FX_FEE:S DEBIT S amount`, `EXT:REVENUE:S CREDIT S amount`. From a
system wallet: the wallet is debited through `AccountApi`, and the journal is `9000000001:NGN DEBIT NGN amount`,
`EXT:REVENUE:NGN CREDIT NGN amount` (VAT and stamp duty credit `EXT:TAX_PAYABLE:NGN`).

- `transfer`: currency-aware `TransferService` and `TransferSettlementService` (the per-payout settling of Paystack

- `fx`: transfer quote creation and confirmation (a new `TransferQuoteService` beside `QuoteService`); `QuoteMath`

- `settlement` (the empty module): `domain`, `repository`, `service` (batches, matching, positions, sweeps),

- `ledger`: new accounts and journal types through `recordJournal`; position queries; kobo methods retired.

### 7.4 Payout state

```
confirm: quote ACTIVE -> EXECUTING (one conditional update)
reserve tx: debit + J1 + remittance PENDING   (commit)
call Flutterwave (no transaction open)
   accepted/processing ............ PENDING
   timeout ........................ PENDING   (re-query, never refund)
   rejected ....................... J4 refund -> FAILED, quote FAILED
settle (webhook | verify | job), only on PENDING (or RETRYING when retries are on):
   success, no conversion ......... J3a -> SUCCESSFUL, quote COMPLETED
   success, with conversion ....... J3b (+ J6 in OWN_LOCK) -> SUCCESSFUL, quote COMPLETED
   failed ......................... J4 -> FAILED, quote FAILED
retries on only:
   converted, not paid ............ J2 -> RETRYING (attempt n of 3, same-currency payout)
   third retry failed ............. J5 -> FAILED, quote FAILED
```

## 8. Constraints

- Money is whole minor units (`long`). Rates and decimals are `BigDecimal`, never `double` or `float`.
- Every balance change writes balanced ledger lines per currency in the same transaction. Lock the sender's and
  recipient's accounts and wallets in the one agreed order (account row, then wallets sorted), so opposite transfers
  never deadlock.
- **No database transaction is open during an HTTP call** (the rate lookup, Flutterwave, or a statement fetch).
- Every transfer and payout needs an `Idempotency-Key`; the same key and request returns the stored result; the same
  key with a different request is refused (422).
- A confirmed quote's rate and fee do not change.
- Never log secrets, webhook secrets, full account numbers or full bank details.
- Flyway owns the schema; never edit an applied migration. `audit_log` and `ledger_entries` stay append-only.
- Modules talk only through `api/` packages. The hooks in `.claude/` (module rule, money types, migration and secret
  protection) must pass.
- Tests never call a real service. The fake Flutterwave gateway behaves by pattern, like the fake Paystack one.
- Test mode, free-tier hosting, no real money.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

**Transfers**
1. **Same currency.** Customer A funds 500 USD and sends 100 USD to B, who holds a USD wallet. A drops by exactly 100,
   B rises by exactly 100, there is no fee, and both are notified.
2. **Cross currency.** A sends USD and chooses NGN for B. The quote shows rate, fee (0 and `PROMO` in the first 30
   days), the exact naira B receives and a 30-minute expiry. Confirming moves both balances by exactly the quoted
   amounts.
3. **Cannot receive.** Choosing a currency B cannot receive is refused with the accepted list. A parked wallet counts as
   receivable.
4. **Lookup.** The lookup returns B's name and currencies, no balances, and is limited per minute.
5. **Fee window.** For a sender 29 days after sign-up the cross-currency fee is 0 (`PROMO`); after 30 days it is 0.5% of
   the source amount rounded up (`STANDARD`). A same-currency transfer is free at any age.
6. **Stamp duty.** An NGN transfer of N10,000 or more still adds N50; a USD transfer adds none.
7. **Limits.** NGN-to-NGN limits are unchanged. A USD or cross-currency transfer above $1,000, or a day above $5,000, is
   refused. With stale rates, non-NGN transfers are refused with the rates message and NGN-to-NGN still works.
8. **Single use.** Confirming one quote twice (including two simultaneous requests) transfers once.
9. **Failure.** A confirmed cross-currency transfer that fails leaves both balances unchanged, marks the quote `FAILED`,
   notifies the sender ("did not go through, no money moved"), and cannot be reused. A refusal before confirm sends no
   notification.
10. **Ledger.** Every journal balances per currency; reconciliation reports no differences after any of the above.
11. **Migration.** `transfers` is `_minor` with a currency; the existing suite passes with no change to its
    assertions; the five kobo ledger methods are gone.

**Payouts**
12. **Remittance.** A GBP customer sends 100 GBP to a Nigerian bank account. The quote shows rate, 1% fee (minimum $1
    equivalent) and the exact naira received; confirming debits GBP once and the payout reaches `SUCCESSFUL`.
13. **Payout abroad, foreign wallet.** A GBP customer pays a UK bank in GBP with no conversion and no Flutterwave rate;
    the fee still applies.
14. **Payout abroad, NGN wallet.** A Nigerian customer pays a bank in another country. With the Nigeria-outbound rule off,
    the quote is refused with `CORRIDOR_NOT_ALLOWED`; with it on, it works. Remittance to Nigeria is unaffected by that
    switch.
15. **Purpose.** A payout without a purpose from the list is refused.
16. **Failure.** A failed payout refunds the sender in full at once (J4), fee included; the payout and quote are
    `FAILED`; no retry runs (retries off).
17. **Retries (only if enabled, O20).** With `retry.enabled` true, a payout that failed after conversion is retried 3
    times from a new same-currency payout; if all fail the sender is refunded in full (J5) and the leftover
    destination-currency amount shows in the settlement position. With it false, this path never runs.
18. **Timeout.** A timed-out payout stays `PENDING`, nothing is refunded, and a later verify or webhook settles it once.
19. **Idempotency.** Retrying the same request with the same key pays once; no Flutterwave request is replayed.
20. **Webhook.** A wrongly signed or unsigned webhook is refused and changes nothing.
21. **Unchanged.** Local NGN-to-NGN payouts through Paystack, their fake-gateway patterns and refunds behave as before.
21a. **Rate lock.** A payout needing conversion shows the rate and the exact recipient amount. In `OWN_LOCK` mode the
    rate and amount stay fixed for 30 minutes even if Flutterwave's rate moves, and a worse real cost is carried by
    PayBridge and posted by J6. In `PROVIDER_LOCK` mode the expiry is the shorter of 30 minutes and Flutterwave's, and the
    confirmed payout sends the stored rate id. If no Flutterwave rate can be obtained the quote is refused and no
    mid-market rate is shown.
21b. **Real verification.** With Flutterwave answering, the quote shows the account name Flutterwave returned, source
    `FLUTTERWAVE`. An account Flutterwave says does not exist refuses the quote and does not fall back.
21c. **Simulated fallback.** With an unsupported corridor, an unavailable Flutterwave or a spent allowance, `auto` mode
    returns a `TEST ACCOUNT <last 4>` name labelled "name not verified (test mode)", stored as `SIMULATED`. Starting the
    app with a simulating mode and a live key fails.
21d. **Shared daily total.** Five transfers or payouts of $1,000 USD-equivalent each (any mix) on one Lagos day are
    allowed; a further $1 is refused for exceeding the $5,000 daily total. A conversion that day does not count toward
    it, and a single $1,001 payout is refused for exceeding the per-transaction limit.

**Settlement**
22. **Positions.** The report shows each pool, fee, in-flight, paid-out and system account per currency, derived from the
    ledger; a pool below its threshold is flagged and nobody is blocked.
23. **Batches.** The daily run produces one batch per partner, currency and UTC day; running the same day twice changes
    nothing; a transfer at 00:30 Lagos time falls in the previous UTC batch.
24. **Matching.** A deliberately missing, mismatched and still-pending payout each appear as an exception of the right
    type, and a matching payout does not.
25. **Pending.** A `PENDING` payout is reported `STILL_PENDING` and is not refunded by the run.
26. **Resolve.** An operator resolves an exception with a note; it is stored and written to `audit_log`; nothing is
    edited.
27. **Sweep.** Sweeping the fee accounts brings their position to zero, increases revenue and tax-payable by the same
    amounts, and reconciliation stays clean.
28. **Operators only.** Without the admin key every settlement endpoint is refused; with no key configured it is 404.

## 10. Areas of concern

- **A. Two payments, not one (payouts).** The debit and the payout happen at different times and can end differently.
  The phase journals and the unknown-outcome rule are what keep the ledger correct.
- **B. Rate lock (decided B16, new risks).** Flutterwave documents the rate as real-time and optional, with no lock or
  expiry stated. If its rate id does lock (O1), the quote can embed it and the amount received is exact. If not,
  PayBridge locks its own quote and carries any worse rate between quote and payout (`OWN_LOCK`, J6). The exposure is
  up to 30 minutes of rate movement on every payout. If the rate cannot be obtained the payout quote is refused, never
  priced at the mid-market rate (R-PAY-3b).
- **B2. Verification can be simulated (decided B18).** A simulated name must never be mistaken for a real one: it is
  labelled to the sender, stored as `SIMULATED`, and the app refuses to start with simulation on and a live key.
- **C. Regulation.** The CBN's revised IMTO guidelines (31 January 2024) are reported to limit licensed operators to
  inbound transfers paid in naira; one explainer gives 1 May 2026 for the Naira-only and settlement-account rules; a
  Substack summary citing Nairametrics says banks and fintechs were separately barred from international transfer
  services. These are secondary sources; the CBN circulars must be read. Nigeria-outbound is ON in test mode as a
  learning choice and not as a statement that it is allowed. Not legal advice.
- **D. The Flutterwave sandbox is not confirmed.** The docs describe mock bank transfers and simulated webhooks, but the
  pages read do not say cross-currency payouts behave in the sandbox, and account resolve may work for Nigerian accounts
  only. The fake gateway is the fallback (O8).
- **E. Rate exposure.** A 30-minute lock with no transfer margin and a 1% payout fee may not cover a rate move or
  Flutterwave's real cost. Acceptable in test mode only (Spec 001 concern E).
- **F. Stale rates stop non-NGN transfers and payouts**, because limits and the $1 minimum need a rate.
- **G. UTC against Lagos days.** Limits follow the Lagos day, settlement the UTC day. They differ by one hour; the batch
  shows its UTC start and end.
- **H. Sweeping system accounts touches real wallets.** The fee, VAT and stamp-duty accounts hold NGN wallet balances.
  A sweep must debit the wallet and post the journal together.
- **I. Retiring the kobo methods.** Paystack payouts use three of them. Re-implementing them through `recordJournal`
  must produce identical lines, proven by the unchanged reconciliation and payout tests. Do it last.
- **J. Lookup as an enumeration tool.** It reveals a name and receivable currencies for an account number. It is rate
  limited and shows no balances.
- **K. Bank details differ by country** (IBAN, sort code, routing, SWIFT). A wrong field is the most likely payout
  failure.
- **L. Retries leave the sender waiting** with the money already out of the wallet. Three attempts and the spacing bound
  it.
- **M. Fee against competitiveness.** The World Bank's average cost of sending $200 to sub-Saharan Africa was 7.9% (Q2
  2023, an old figure), against a 3% target for 2030. 1% may be below provider cost.
- **N. Idempotency across two systems.** Our reference must reach Flutterwave as the transfer `reference` on every attempt
  (with an attempt suffix when retries are on) so a restart or retry never pays twice.
